## Goal
Design a replacement for the Regal center in Revit.

## Solution
Yolf, a 3D minigolf course with a bass pro shops style store.

### What is 3D minigolf
3D minigolf is the same as regular minigolf but with height amplified courses.

### Why is it called "Yolf"
Due to the amplified height and the fact that in many cases the Y axis is up and down I chose Yolf as the name for this building. Although I do guess I could have called it Zolf but that's besides the point.

## Exterior Design
Use of angles to convey motion and activity. Large curtain walls to light interior.
