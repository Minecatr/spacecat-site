## Overview
Operation: Sky is a survival game where you spawns on a small island in the sky and must harvest points from the source, a mysterious glowing white cube. These points can be used to purchase upgrades that enhance your island. These upgrades range from letting your island grow trees to making the source produce more points. There are also various different islands to discover, 
each unlocking new sets of resources which expand on the games features.

### Controls
Move - WASD
Use Item/Interact - LClick
Jump - Space
Move Camera - RClick
