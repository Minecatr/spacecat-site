## Overview
Rex Chunga Simulator 2 is the continuation of the original Rex Chunga Simulator. In this game you play as Rex, a very hungry dog, after your mom leaves to go on a trip you break loose into town and must eat everything to become the biggest of dogs.

### Controls
Move - WASD
Jump - Space
Move Camera - LMB

### Notes
This game was created using the Godot engine.
